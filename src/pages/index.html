    VerumVeil · three ciphers, 2024

    Can a secret sit in plain sight, in a row of faces, and stay a secret?

    Method

    Three ways to hide in plain sight. AI Sentence Crypt turns your message into an ordinary sentence that means something else. SkipCode, borrowed from Sherlock's The Empty Hearse, buries it among innocent words. Emoji Crypt, the one here, turns it into faces. The password decides everything.

    Readings

    Honest result: Emoji Crypt is a substitution cipher. It hides a short note from a glance, but frequency analysis would crack a long one. GitHub ↗

    Instrument

    Runs in your browser. Change the password and try to open it.

    1Ld

    Last Drink · in design, name not final

    Can a drinking app reward going slower instead of drinking more?

    Method

    Points stop at hourly, nightly and weekly caps. Water between rounds earns points. There is no progress bar to chase, and alerts flag sudden spikes. Safeguards borrowed from gambling, from day one.

    Why me

    Five years behind London bars. I have watched the third drink turn into the sixth more times than I can count.

    Instrument

    0points tonight

    Start the night. Two an hour earn points. Water counts only between drinks.
