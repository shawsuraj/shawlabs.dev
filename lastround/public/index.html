Good nights,

A drink tracker for nights out. Log any drink in two taps, earn points for a good pace and for water, and wake up to a receipt and a map of where the night went.

Your nights stay on your phone. No account, no ads, nothing sent to us.

Join the alpha

House rules

Three, and they are short.

  1. It stays on your phone. No account, no ads, nothing sent to us. On iPhone the map comes from Apple Maps; that is the only thing that ever goes online.
  2. Water earns points. Have a water between drinks and tap Water. It scores from ten minutes after your last drink.
  3. Two taps a drink. Tap Drink, tap what you are having. Same again is one.

How the points work

  • A good pace scores. Each drink earns points, and so does each water break in between.
  • There are caps, by the hour, the night and the week. Past one, points simply stop. You will never see a number, a bar or a countdown to a cap, because a visible ceiling becomes a target.
  • You can set your own weekly limit. It caps your points too, and it cannot be switched off on a whim mid-night.
  • Alerts wait for a pause. At most one an hour, each at most once a night. They read as care, never as a telling-off.
  • The morning after, you get a receipt. Where you went, how long you were out, your water breaks and your points.

In the alpha

What you get on night one.

A map of your nights

Every bar you have had a night in, with how many nights. A soft amber glow spreads over the streets you go out in. Tap a bar for its receipts, or Drink here to start. iPhone first; Android gets a list of your places.

Pick the bar

Start a night and the bars near you are already there, with the ones you have been to before at the top. Or just type the name.

A calendar, not a streak

Your weeks, months and year at a glance, shaded amber by the night, with a teal dot for water. No streak to keep alive.

The receipt

The morning after, a printed receipt with your name on it: where you went, how long you were out, water breaks and points.

Share a night

Turn a night into a picture for your story. Place, time out, water breaks. Never how many drinks.

Care, not nagging

A reminder of the plan you set and a water nudge, both off until you turn them on. Links to real help are always one tap away.

On the bench

Being built during the alpha.

  • The night moves with you. Tap Moved bar and the night becomes a route: every bar, in order, on the receipt and the map.
  • Passport stamps. A stamp for each new bar, city and country, and a globe that fills in amber where you have had a night out.
  • Coasters. A coaster for every bar you visit, its design drawn from the bar's name, so no two are the same.
  • Behind the bar. A short card on what is in your drink and where it comes from, written by someone who poured them for years.
  • The morning outlook. One honest line on the receipt, like "A slow start likely. Water and breakfast first."
  • Never open the app. Same again and water from the Lock Screen, a Live Activity with the bar and time out, and Siri: "add a Guinness".

Some of these may land a build or two into the alpha. Testers get every build as it ships.

What testers help answer

  • Do the caps feel fair? Or do they get in the way of a good night?
  • Do points get people drinking water? The whole idea rests on this one.
  • Do the alerts feel like care? Or like being told off?
  • Is the receipt worth opening the morning after?

No analytics, so nothing is learned behind your back. Two short forms, after week one and week four, and a group chat for bugs. That is how the app gets better.

What it will never do

  • Rank, reward or show off how much anyone drinks.
  • Show a countdown, bar or ring towards a cap.
  • Send your nights anywhere, or write them to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Put you on a map for anyone else. No friends on the map, no live location.
  • Track you. No analytics, no ads, no account.

Where it is

Now

Alpha

Recruiting now. A small group of testers, let in a few at a time, for four weeks of real nights out. iPhone through TestFlight, Android through Play testing, both from the same build.

Next

Beta

  • Water you can prove. Your camera checks the water break, on the phone itself, and the clip is deleted straight after.
  • A bar passport. A stamp for every bar, with your first visit and how many nights you have spent there.
  • Split the G. Rate the pour, and see how close you get to the G. A bit of fun for Guinness drinkers.
  • Wrapped. Your year of nights out, the places and the water breaks, made to share.
  • Smarter nights. "Still at The Crown?" when you open the app somewhere new, and maybe, if you switch it on, a nudge when you leave a bar to log the next one or get home well.
  • Little extras. What the night cost, if you want to know, and badges for a good night: three water breaks, home by the plan, a new place.
