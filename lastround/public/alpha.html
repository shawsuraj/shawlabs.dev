Alpha terms

Thank you for testing Last Round. By installing the alpha, you agree to the following.

  • You are 18 or over. Last Round tracks drinking and is for adults only.
  • This is a test build. It may have bugs, and it may lose your data. Do not rely on it for anything that matters.
  • Drink responsibly. Points are a game, not a goal. Never drink more to score, and never drive after drinking.
  • No guarantees. The alpha is given as it is, and it is not medical advice. If you are worried about your drinking, see Help.
  • Your data stays yours. Everything stays on your phone, as the privacy policy sets out.
  • You can stop at any time. Use Delete everything, then remove the app.

Reporting a bug

Send a screenshot and what you tapped in the alpha WhatsApp group, or email shaw@shawsuraj.com. Every bug becomes a test before it is fixed.
