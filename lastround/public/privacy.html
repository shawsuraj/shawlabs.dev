Privacy

Everything you log in Last Round stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. This page says exactly what is kept, where, and the one thing that talks to Apple Maps on iPhone.

Who we are

Last Round is made by Shaw Labs, run by Suraj Shaw in London. For any privacy question, email lastround@shawlabs.dev.

What the app keeps, only on your phone

  • Each drink and water break, with its time.
  • The place you type for a night or, on iPhone, the bar you pick from the suggestions, with where that bar is (its map point and Apple Maps' id for it). And your plan, such as the time you mean to be home.
  • Your name, if you set one. It is printed on your receipts and never on a picture you share.
  • Your weekly limit, which alerts you have seen, and how long you keep your nights.
  • For your age, a yes and when you tapped it. Never your date of birth.

What we never collect

No account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. No contacts and no health data. Nothing is sent to a server of ours, because there is none. Where you are is never stored. On Android the app has no internet or location permission.

Bar suggestions and the map (iPhone only)

On iPhone you can turn on Suggest bars near me in You, or by answering yes the first time you open the Map tab. It is off until you do. When it is on and you start a night or open the Map tab, the app asks iPhone for your location once and uses Apple Maps to list bars near you. If you search, what you type goes to Apple Maps too, and tapping one of Apple's bar labels or Search this area asks Apple about that place or that part of the map.

Apple handles that request under Apple's own privacy policy. It does not come to us, and your location is never saved: only the bar you pick, and where that bar is, is kept with that night on your phone. Delete everything removes it, and the setting goes back to off. Android does not have this feature and asks for no location.

How far each suggested bar is shows on screen only and is never saved. The map on iPhone (the Map tab, and the map from the Start sheet) is drawn by Apple Maps, so the area you are looking at is sent to Apple to draw it, even with suggestions off. With suggestions on, opening the map reads your location once and marks where you were. The map never follows you, and the mark goes when you leave the map. Your map is drawn from the bars you picked, kept on your phone.

Backups

On iPhone, the app's data is kept out of iCloud and Finder backups. On Android, app backup is turned off. So your nights are not copied to any cloud.

How long nights are kept

Whole nights older than the period you pick are deleted on the phone. You can choose 30 days, 3 months, 6 months or 12 months. It is 12 months unless you change it. Your most recent night stays until a newer one, or until you delete everything.

Your controls

  • Delete everything wipes the phone's copy at once. One thing stays for up to a day: a weekly limit you set yourself. That is on purpose, so deleting cannot be used to get around a limit you chose.
  • Export my nights makes a JSON file, your name included, that you save wherever you choose. That copy is then yours to keep or delete.

Notifications

The plan reminder and the water nudge are both off until you turn them on. They are set on your phone, not sent from a server, and never name a drink.

Sharing a night

Share your night makes a picture on your phone, only when you tap it, and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you. It never shows your name, your points or a limit.

Links out

The Drinkaware, NHS, Drinkline and Samaritans links in the app open in your browser or phone app. Last Round sends nothing with them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Age

Last Round is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not for children.

Our legal basis

Because nothing you log ever reaches us, we do not collect or process it, and we are not a controller of the data kept on your phone. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you, and we delete them when the conversation is over.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. Its fonts are served from this site. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time to run and secure the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and testers hear about it in the alpha group before the build that needs it.

18+ only. Last Round is not medical advice. If you are worried about your drinking, help is here.
