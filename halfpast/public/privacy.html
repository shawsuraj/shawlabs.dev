Privacy

Everything you log in Half Past stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. This page says exactly what is kept, where, and what the map and bar search send to Apple or Google.

Who we are

Half Past is made by Shaw Labs, run by Suraj Shaw in London. For any privacy question, email halfpast@shawlabs.dev.

What the app keeps, only on your phone

  • Each drink and water break, with its time.
  • The place you type for a night, or the bar you pick from the suggestions, with where that bar is: its map point and the map service's id for it (Apple Maps' on iPhone, Google's on Android). And your plan, such as the time you mean to be home.
  • Your name, if you set one. It is printed on your receipts and never on a picture you share.
  • Your weekly limit, which alerts you have seen, and how long you keep your nights.
  • For your age, a yes and when you tapped it. Never your date of birth.

What we never collect

No account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. No contacts and no health data. Nothing is sent to a server of ours, because there is none. Where you are is never stored.

Bar suggestions and the map on iPhone

You can turn on Suggest bars near me in You, or by answering yes the first time you open the Map tab. It is off until you do. When it is on and you start a night or open the Map tab, the app asks iPhone for your location once and uses Apple Maps to list bars near you. During a night, coming back to the app checks your location once more to ask if you have moved on to a new bar. That check stays on your phone and is sent to no one.

If you search, what you type goes to Apple Maps too, and Search this area asks Apple about that part of the map. Apple handles those requests under Apple's own privacy policy. They do not come to us, and your location is never saved: only the bar you pick, and where that bar is, is kept with that night on your phone. Delete everything removes it, and the setting goes back to off.

How far each suggested bar is shows on screen only and is never saved. The map (the Map tab, and the map from the Start sheet) is drawn by Apple Maps, so the area you are looking at is sent to Apple to draw it, even with suggestions off. With suggestions on, opening the map reads your location once and marks where you were. The map never follows you, and the mark goes when you leave the map. Your map is drawn from the bars you picked, kept on your phone.

Bar suggestions and the map on Android

It works the same way, with Google in Apple's place. The first time a search needs your location, Android asks whether Half Past may use it while the app is open, and you can choose approximate. Your location and anything you type to search go to Google's Places API to find bars. The map is drawn by Google Maps through Google Play services, so the area you are looking at is sent to Google, even with suggestions off. Google handles those requests under Google's own privacy policy, and Google Play services works under Google's own terms. None of it comes to us.

Notice when I leave a bar

This is off until you turn it on, and it needs notifications and location set to Always. While a night is open, your phone keeps one circle round tonight's bar and wakes Half Past only when you walk out of it, to send one notification that names no place, drink or number. On iPhone the circle is kept by iOS. On Android it is kept by Google Play services, under a plain name with no time or place in it. The circle is removed when the night ends. A night that closes itself while the app is away sends nothing, and its circle goes the next time you open the app or walk out. Where you go is never kept.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri, what you say is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, and Apple keeps its own history of it. Half Past cannot reach that history. The app keeps no record of a drink you named to Siri beyond the drink itself in your night.

Backups

On iPhone, the app's data is kept out of iCloud and Finder backups. On Android, app backup is turned off. So your nights are not copied to any cloud.

How long nights are kept

Whole nights older than the period you pick are deleted on the phone. You can choose 30 days, 3 months, 6 months or 12 months. It is 12 months unless you change it. Your most recent night stays until a newer one, or until you delete everything.

Your controls

  • Delete everything wipes the phone's copy at once. One thing stays for up to a day: a weekly limit you set yourself. That is on purpose, so deleting cannot be used to get around a limit you chose.
  • Export my nights makes a JSON file, your name included, that you save wherever you choose. That copy is then yours to keep or delete.

Notifications

The plan reminder and the water nudge are both off until you turn them on. They are set on your phone, not sent from a server, and never name a drink.

Sharing a night

Share your night makes a picture on your phone, only when you tap it, and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you. It never shows your name, your points or a limit.

Links out

The Drinkaware, NHS, Drinkline and Samaritans links in the app open in your browser or phone app. Half Past sends nothing with them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Age

Half Past is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not for children.

If you sign up for the alpha

This is the one place we hold anything about you. The sign-up form asks for:

  • Your name and email address. Use the email on your Apple ID or Google account, because that is where the test invite goes.
  • Whether you are on iPhone or Android.
  • Your Instagram name, only if you choose to give it.
  • A yes that you are 18 or over, and a yes that you are happy to get emails about the alpha.

We use it only to run the alpha: to send your invite through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play, to tell you about new builds and changes to these terms, and to send two short feedback forms, one after your first week and one at the end. We do not send marketing, and we never share or sell your details.

When you press Sign up, your answers go to a private Google Sheet kept by Suraj Shaw, through Google Apps Script, and a short note about the sign-up is emailed to halfpast@shawlabs.dev. Google handles that under Google's own terms. To send your invite, your email also goes to Apple or Google, whichever your phone uses. Nothing you log in the app is ever added to it.

We delete your details when the alpha ends, unless you ask to join the beta. You can see what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted at any time: email halfpast@shawlabs.dev. Leaving the alpha does not affect anything on your phone.

Our legal basis

Because nothing you log ever reaches us, we do not collect or process it, and we are not a controller of the data kept on your phone. For the alpha sign-up, we are the controller, and we rely on your consent, which you give with the two ticks on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you, and we delete them when the conversation is over.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. Its fonts are served from this site. The sign-up form sends nothing until you press Sign up, and then only what you typed, to Google as set out above. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time to run and secure the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and testers hear about it in the alpha group before the build that needs it.
